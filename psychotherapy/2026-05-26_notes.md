## Anxiety → Anger

Ivan's own formulation: **"если мне тревожно, ТО я злюсь"**  
(If I'm anxious, THEN I get angry)

Masha confirmed: "по большей части" (for the most part)

This is a direct conditional, not a gradual sequence. Anxiety doesn't slowly convert into anger — it immediately produces it. The "то" matters.

Theoretical frame: secondary emotion (EFT, Greenberg) — anger fires as the direct response to anxiety. Anxiety is the trigger, anger is the output.

Implication: catching the anxiety early enough means recognizing that what follows will be anger. The question worth exploring: what is the anxiety actually about before it becomes anger?
